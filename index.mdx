Toban Contribution Viewer is an AI-powered analytics platform that connects to your team's digital workspaces, collects activity data from Slack (and optionally GitHub and Notion), and surfaces actionable contribution insights through LLM analysis and a React dashboard. It helps teams recognize hidden contributors, understand communication patterns, and make data-driven decisions.

## What Toban Does

Toban connects to your team's Slack workspace through OAuth, periodically syncs messages and reactions into a PostgreSQL database, and then uses OpenRouter (supporting Claude, GPT-4, and other LLMs) to analyze those messages and generate structured insights: topic summaries, key contributor profiles, discussion highlights, and cross-channel reports.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your team">
    Sign in with Supabase Auth and create a team. Invite members and assign roles (owner, admin, or member).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Slack">
    Add a Slack integration by completing the OAuth flow — enter your Slack app credentials in the UI, then authorize workspace access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select channels and sync">
    Choose which channels to analyze. Toban syncs messages and thread replies into its database on demand or on a schedule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run analysis">
    Trigger an LLM analysis on any synced channel. Toban returns topic breakdowns, contributor insights, and key highlights powered by your chosen model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate reports">
    Combine multiple channel analyses into a cross-resource report for a holistic view of team contribution across your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Features

- **Slack OAuth** — zero-config workspace connection; credentials entered in the UI, not environment variables
- **Background sync** — channel and message sync runs in FastAPI background tasks to avoid timeouts
- **LLM flexibility** — switch between Claude, GPT-4, and other OpenRouter models per analysis
- **Role-based access** — owner, admin, and member roles control who can create integrations and generate reports
- **Cross-resource reports** — aggregate insights across multiple Slack channels in a single report
- **Supabase authentication** — JWT-based auth with email, GitHub, and Google sign-in support

<Note>
Toban is designed to be self-hosted. You bring your own Supabase project, PostgreSQL database, OpenRouter API key, and Slack app credentials. See the [Self-Hosting guide](/self-hosting) to get started.
</Note>
